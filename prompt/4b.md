xr_{t:t+H}^{H} = sum from h=1 to H of xr_{t+h}^{H-h+1} where t+1 means 12-month lag later of the same month and t+h means h-year lag later of the same month.

(1/H) xr_{t:t+H}^{H} = a^{H} + b^{H} * xy_{t}^{H} + e_{t}^{H}

Calculate b^{H} and t-stat using Hansen & Hodrick method:
- Define the regressor vector x_t = [1, xy_{t}^{H}]' 
- Q_hat = 1/T * sum from t=1 to T of x_t x_t'
- Omega_hat_0 = 1/T * sum from t=1 to T of e_t^2 x_t x_t'
- For lag = 0,...,L, Omega_hat_l = 1/(T-l) * sum from t=l+1 to T of (e_t x_t)(e_{t-l} x_{t-l})'
- S_HAC =   Omega_hat_0 + sum from l=1 to L of (Omega_hat_l + Omega_hat_l') 
- Impose L = Overlap = 12H-1 
- Var = (1/T)* Q_hat^(-1) * S_HAC * Q_hat^(-1)

Report b^{H}, t-stat, and R-squared for each H. Create a Latex table, use 1 row for each maturity H = 2,3,4,5 years. Round numbers to 4 decimal places. Output: code/4b.R, figures/4b.tex
